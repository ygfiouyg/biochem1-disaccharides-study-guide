4.  Acid hydrolysis: Procedure:
1.  Add 5 ml of sucrose, in a test tube
2.  add 2 ml of dilute sulfuric acid.
3.  Boil for 2 minutes then Cool thoroughly under the tap.
4.  Neutralize by adding 2 ml of 30% NaOH.
5.  Take about 2 ml of the hydrolysate and add 2ml of the mixed
    Fehling's reagent and boil.
Observation: Red ppt
• N.B:It is essential to neutralize the hydrolysate with NaOH
  before applying Fehling's solution as excess acid will dissolve
  cuprous oxide.
• Comment:
• The acid hydrolyses sucrose to glucose and fructose & both of
  them reduce Cu+2 to Cu2O (red ppt).
